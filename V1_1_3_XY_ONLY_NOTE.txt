euSKlid V1.1.3 - XY-only simplification

Base: accepted V1.1.2 master-first/axial reference.

Changes:
- U/V frame overlay removed; show_uv_frame is kept as a compatibility no-op.
- FreeCAD X/Y/Z axis-cross is no longer hidden/restored by euSKlid.
- New and legacy sketch data are interpreted on FreeCAD XY.
- Axis-parallel labels are X/Y; legacy U/V internal names remain accepted as aliases.
