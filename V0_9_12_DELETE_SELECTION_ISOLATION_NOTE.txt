euSKlidWB V0.9.12 DELETE SELECTION ISOLATION
- Internal euSKlid entity selection is now isolated from FreeCAD object selection.
- Open Sketch/New Sketch no longer leave the full sketch object selected in FreeCAD.
- Shift+click clears FreeCAD selection and only toggles internal euSKlid entity selection.
- Delete/Suppr clears FreeCAD selection before deleting selected internal entities.
- This prevents FreeCAD native Delete from deleting the whole sketch object/entities unexpectedly.
