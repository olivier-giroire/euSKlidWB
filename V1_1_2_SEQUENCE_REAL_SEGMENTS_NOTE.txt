euSKlidWB V1.1.2 - sequence constraints on real segments

- No auxiliary/construction segments are created for sequenced parallel-line spacing.
- Tangency constraints are preserved.
- Collinearity constraints between segments from the same construction line are preserved.
- For each repeated spacing family, the first actual contour segment is the master:
  * optional Horizontal/Vertical on the master only
  * one Distance dimension on the master
- Following actual contour segments receive Equal to the master.
