euSKlidWB V1.0.3 SEQUENCE PARSER FINAL
- Base retained: V1.0.2 EXPORT_SEQUENCE_EQUAL.
- Distance list separator is comma only; spaces around commas are optional.
- Sequence syntax uses parentheses: repeat 13 (0,1) origin 0 step 2.5.
- Sequence must start with repeat/rep/r only.
- Origin aliases: origin/org/o.
- Step aliases: step/stp/s.
- Mixed expressions are supported: repeat 13 (0,1) org 0 step 2.5, repeat 6 (0,2) o 40 s 8.
- Old [] sequence notation and stp-as-sequence-start are intentionally rejected.
